EMBER CLASH

Duel of the Four Embers

Four elements. One deck. Thirty life.
Spend ◆ mana to play cards — strike, shield, heal, draw.
Play two cards of the same element in one turn for +2 Resonance damage.
Reduce the Cinder Warden to 0 life before yours runs out.

Mouse or keyboard: 1–7 play a card · E end turn · M sound

How to play

  • Each turn you gain +1 max mana (up to 10) and draw a card.
  • Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it if you can afford its cost.
  • 🔥 Ember hits hard. 💧 Tide draws cards. 🌿 Thorn heals. ⬢ Stone shields.
  • Resonance: two cards of the same element in one turn — the second deals +2 damage.
  • Damage hits shields first, then life. First to 0 life loses.
  • Press E (or End Turn) when done. The Warden plays, then it is your turn again.
